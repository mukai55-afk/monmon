How Do You Say That in English?

Short, natural English phrases for everyday Japanese expressions using water.

Back
水を汲む → draw water or fetch water
  1. She drew water from the well.
  2. He fetched water from the river.
  3. They drew water for cooking.
水を掬う → scoop up water
  1. She scooped up water with her hands.
  2. The child scooped up water from the pond.
  3. He used a cup to scoop up water.
水をかける → pour water on or splash water on
  1. She poured water on the flowers.
  2. He splashed water on his face.
  3. The kids splashed water on each other.
水をやる → water or give water to
  1. I watered the plants this morning.
  2. She gives water to the dog every day.
  3. He watered the garden after school.
水を入れる → put water in or fill with water
  1. I filled the pool with water in the morning.
  2. She put water in the vase.
  3. He filled the bottle with water.
水に入る → go into the water or get in the water
  1. The children went into the water.
  2. He got in the water slowly.
  3. We went into the water at the beach.
