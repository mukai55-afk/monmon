Today's phrases 1 → Looks like / Sounds like / I feel like
  1. Looks to me like you were not telling the truth.
  2. Almost looks like he was smiling at me as I took his photo.
  3. Seems like I'm not gonna be able to make it.
  4. Sounds like you've done your homework.
  5. I feel like I can finally give her a meaningful answer.
  6. You look like you've got something on your mind you'd like to share.
  7. Looks like rain. / Sounds like fun.
  8. Looks good. / Sounds interesting.
Today's phrases 2 → There is no way
  1. There is no way I can eat everything now.
  2. There is no way I can ask him that.
  3. There is no way that would happen in the near future.
  4. There is no way we're not gonna bring change to Japan.
  5. There is no way of knowing where he has gone.
  6. There is no way to survive.
  7. There is no specific way to change the fact that everyone sees the world differently.

  1. The thing is, I've tried talking to him, but he just won't listen.

  5. The issue is, he doesn't know how to apologize.
  6. The truth is, I'm just not ready.
  7. The fact is, she already made her decision.
  8. The catch is, it only works once.
  9. The bottom line is, we need more funding.
  10. The point is, we can't give up now.

Today's phrases 9 → hope / expect / wish
  1. I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow.
  2. I hope she is safe.
  3. I hope it won't rain. = I want clear skies.
  4. I expect it will rain. = I think rain is likely.
  5. I hope you have a good trip.
  6. I expect it will rain tomorrow.
  7. I expect him to be late.
  8. I expect sales to improve next year.
  9. I wish I were taller.
  10. I wish I had studied harder.
  11. I wish it would stop raining.

Today's phrases 15 → That is why / That is how
  1. He studied hard every day. That is why he passed the exam.
  2. He studied hard every day. That is how he passed the exam.
  3. She didn't sleep enough. That is why she was tired.
  4. It was raining heavily. That is why the game was canceled.
  5. She was nervous. That is why she couldn't speak well.
  6. He saved money for years. That is why he could buy a house.
  7. She went to bed early every night. That is how she stayed healthy.
  8. She listened carefully. That is how she understood the lesson.
  9. We followed the instructions. That is how we built the table.
  10. I saved a little money each month. That is how I bought my PC.
