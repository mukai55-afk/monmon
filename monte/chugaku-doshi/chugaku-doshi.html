トンチンカン +場所 +時 +when, if, because, so that +トンチンカン
1

チン（動詞）

現在・過去・未来・進行・完了・大過去

I told you so. I am telling you so. I will tell you so. I am gonna tell you so. I have told you so. I had told you already.
2

チン（助動詞＋動詞）

may / can / should / will / must
be able to / ought to / be going to / have to
might / could / would

He must tell her so.

He must have told her so. ～したに違いない

He should have told her so. ①～したはずだ　②～すべきだった

3

チン（受動態）

カン（が）～される

Everyoneloves him in Japan.

→

He is loved by everyone.

Everyonewill love

→

He will be loved by everyone.

Everyonehas loved

→

He has been loved by everyone.

→ will have been loved

Everyoneis criticizing

→

He is being criticized by everyone.

→ will be being criticized

4

チン（他動詞 vs. 自動詞）

He changed trains at the station.

Core Concept

状態

She is a doctor. （名詞）である

She is tired. （形容詞）である

She knows it. （動詞）である ［状態動詞］

[be 動詞]（状態 / ＝）
amiswasbeenbeingarewere

She is speaking English. （動詞）してる状態である［動作動詞］

→

She speaks English. （動詞）する習慣・事実

Core Concept

変化

1（トンが）～に変化する

She became a doctor. （名詞）になる

← She is a doctor.

She got tired. （形容詞）になる

← She is tired.

He got killed in a car accident.

← He is killed.

They got married.

← They are married.

She came to know it. （動詞）になる

← She knows it.

to love (it)likehateknowrealize

I began to love him. 変化が始まる

≒ I began loving him. ↔ I stopped loving him.

We were getting hungry.

（だんだん） 変化していく

You will get to like her.

〃

You must learn to think before you speak. （努力して）変化する

（トンが）形容詞に変化する 意味ありげ / cf. get tired

go mad / go bad / go crazy

My dream has come true. / It’ll come right in the end.

The leaves have turned yellow. / His face turned red.

形容詞：注

I gradually became able to speak English. (= came to be able to)

become able to ← 変化be able to ← 状態

I never wanted to become aware of the outside world!

Core Concept 変化

2（カンを）～変化させる

She always makes me laugh.

make：強制

His mother let him play in the park.

let：許可

I had my son clean up his room.

have：当然

How can I get him to quit drinking?

get：説得

My boss forced me to serve everybody coffee.

force：強制

His parents won’t allow him to stay out late.

allow：許可

Can you help me with my work?

Can you help me (to) do my work?

Core Concept 変化

3（トンが）カンの変化を被る

have (get) + カン + 過去分詞

My bag was stolen.

→

I had my bag stolen.

（～される）

Her dog was trained.

→

She had her dog trained.

（～させる）

My hair was cut yesterday.

→

I had my hair cut.

（～してもらう）

cf. He had my breakfast ready（形容詞） then.

He made her a doctor.

He made her happy.

He made her laugh.

VERB FOCUS

change｜変わる / 変える

①（トンが）変わる自動詞

Technology changes so fast these days.

Her mood changed when she heard the news.

He went to Kyoto this summer.

He visited Kyoto this summer.

②（カンを）変える他動詞

She changed her hairstyle before the party.

→

Her hairstyle was changed ...

The internet has changed the way we work.

→

The way we work has been changed ...

He made her a doctor.

→

She was made a doctor by him.

He made her happy.

→

She was made happy by him.

He made her laugh.

→

She was made to laugh by him.

← She was made chief physician by the hospital director.

← She was made happy by him for the first time in years.

← She was made to laugh for the camera.
